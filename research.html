Current work

The foundation of my research path.

M.S. thesis · final semester

01

Auditable Knowledge Tracing with a Temporal Heterogeneous Student Knowledge Map

The thesis develops a knowledge-tracing representation that separates shared structure, mutable learner state, and immutable evidence. It uses train-only provenance and prediction-before-update replay so the state available to a prediction can be reconstructed and challenged.

The native map keeps concept topology fixed while response evidence updates learner state. Its descriptive estimates are distinct from the separate benchmark predictor.

The final evaluation was prospectively locked, and a separate verification workflow reconstructed the frozen release artifacts. The result supports a small, bounded graph contribution and technical auditability; it does not claim universal graph superiority, mastery validity, fairness, teacher usability, or improved learning.

A related graph manuscript, Technically Auditable Knowledge Tracing: A Leakage-Controlled Temporal Student Knowledge Map, was submitted to the International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education in 2026. It is a submitted manuscript, not an accepted publication. The expanded thesis contains additional testing and interpretation beyond that submission.

02

From Prediction to Educational Consequence: An Evidence-to-Action Framework for Adaptive Assessment

This structured critical narrative review synthesizes psychometrics, computerized adaptive testing, learner modeling, knowledge tracing, sequential decision policies, foundation-model-assisted assessment, human factors, and governance.

I am the first and corresponding author, working with Xin Zhong. The manuscript has been submitted to Studies in Educational Evaluation.

The learner-map work makes model state and provenance inspectable. This framework places that technical contribution within a wider evidence chain: an inspectable estimate still needs appropriate validity, decision, human-factors, and outcome evidence before it can justify educational action.

Research questions

What I am trying to understand.

The questions are technical and human at the same time: what the model represents, what its output means, and what action the evidence can actually support.

Representation

What state should a learner model retain?

Preserve temporal order, concept relationships, and provenance without collapsing history into an opaque score.

Evaluation

Which claim does a metric support?

Distinguish prediction, calibration, validity, fairness, instructional effect, and operational usefulness.

Action

Who decides what happens next?

Keep inference, policy, reporting, human authority, and governance visible as separate functions.

Research practice

Evidence before narrative.

Validity

Do not substitute accuracy for meaning.

Predictive performance alone does not establish mastery, fairness, validity, or improved learning.

Reproducibility

Preserve how the decision was reached.

Keep data boundaries, state transitions, comparisons, and verification artifacts available for inspection.

Boundaries

State what the work does not prove.

Strong research is clearer—not weaker—when its contribution and limits are both explicit.

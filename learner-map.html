Read the map

Structure stays. Evidence changes.

A descriptive learner-state display. The estimates summarize recorded responses; they do not establish mastery, learning gains, or an effective tutor.

01 / Structure

Dots are concepts.

There are 188 fixed nodes. Concept labels are relabeled IDs, not invented skill names. The 178 visible links are a sparse projection for readability; the original native calculation uses 15,387 fixed relationships.

02 / State

Responses update evidence.

Direct estimates use cumulative correct and total counts. A response tagged to more than one concept updates each tagged concept once. All 1,001 saved positions, including the initial prior, are retained.

03 / Context

Neighbors can move a value.

The native 80/20 blend combines a concept’s direct estimate with a weighted mean of neighbors’ direct estimates. A smoothed value can change without direct practice. The topology does not grow or adapt during this replay.

Under the surface

Keep the calculation and its meaning separate.

This is the original native descriptive replay, not a new simulation, fitted animation or re-run of the research pipeline.

Direct evidence

d = (1 + s) / (2 + n)

s counts correct responses and n counts observations on a concept. The Beta(1,1) prior starts at 0.50 when n = 0; that is not evidence of 50% mastery. Exact counts and direct estimates are preserved.

Neighbor smoothing

g = 0.8d + 0.2 × mean

The native engine uses weighted neighboring direct estimates. It does not repeatedly smooth neighbors’ smoothed values. A changed g with unchanged n and s is indirect model movement, not a new direct observation.

Order and time

Observe, then update.

Position k is after k recorded interactions and before interaction k + 1 is observed. This native path uses cumulative counts without elapsed-time forgetting. Times are omitted here; playback speed is not a learner’s practice rate.

For a smaller worked example, open the separate synthetic teaching replay. It is supplementary and does not replace this real history.

Interpretation

A changing map is a question, not a verdict.

Check

Ask what changed underneath.

Inspect counts and direct versus smoothed values. Different questions, shared tags, and neighboring evidence can all affect interpretation. A rising direct estimate can reflect easier questions, repeated items or selection—not necessarily learning.

Separate

The benchmark is a different system.

The thesis also evaluates a separate predictive representation using a 70/30 mixture, additional history features, fitted correction and calibration. That predictor is not integrated into this native map display; its traces are not included here.

Bound

Inspectability does not establish validity.

This previously exposed training example demonstrates recorded state behavior. It does not establish generalization, mastery validity, causal learning gains, teacher usefulness, improved instruction or unique graph superiority.

This projection retains every saved native state and fixed coordinate, relabels concept IDs, and omits original identifiers, item aliases, explicit response rows, times, recent-answer arrays, forecast traces and full graph attributes.

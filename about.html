About

From aircraft maintenance to auditable AI.

I’m a researcher, a software builder, and a U.S. Air Force veteran. Right now, I’m finishing my master’s and starting my Ph.D., with AI in education at the center of my work.

Background

A different route into research.

Before focusing on software and research, I maintained B-1B and KC-135 airframes in the United States Air Force. That environment rewarded procedural discipline, careful diagnosis, clear handoffs, and respect for the difference between a plausible explanation and a verified condition.

I apply the same outlook to AI research and software engineering. My work focuses on learner modeling, educational technology, human-centered evaluation, persistent systems, and technical communication.

Good technical work should make it possible to see what happened, why it happened, and where uncertainty remains.

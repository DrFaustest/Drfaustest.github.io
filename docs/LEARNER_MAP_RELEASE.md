## Public explanation and real replay

The central page is `learner-map.html`: the original real 1,000-interaction history, all 1,001 saved positions and 188 concept states. The separate synthetic exercise at `learner-map-synthetic.html` is an optional worked example. Home, Work, Research and Résumé link to the real central page. Unrelated portfolio content and existing design are retained.

The real projection relabels concept IDs; retains exact saved counts, successes, direct estimates, graph-smoothed estimates, fixed coordinates and 178 display-edge endpoints; and omits item aliases, explicit response rows, all times, recent-answer arrays, forecast traces and full weighted graph records. No original learner/event/question ID or manuscript is included. The 178 visible links are a sparse display projection of the original 15,387-edge calculation; those visible links alone cannot reproduce saved smoothed values.

Exact state transitions still reveal aspects of the underlying response pattern. The page does not claim anonymity or absence of response information. EdNet/Riiid attribution, CC BY-NC 4.0 license links, adaptation notice and scientific limits are visible on the page. This is a noncommercial educational research demonstration, not an endorsement or fresh predictive validation.

## Semantics

Native direct state is `(1+s)/(2+n)`; default native smoothing is 80% direct plus 20% weighted neighboring direct estimates. Counts do not decay with elapsed time in this path. The separate benchmark uses its own 70/30 representation, history features, fitted correction and calibration; it is not integrated into this map replay. Node colors are computed estimates, not validated mastery or causal learning gains. The original exposed training history is distinct from the later G01 concept-52 gallery case.
